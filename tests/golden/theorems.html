Definitions

Definition 1. Let \(\ell \ne p\) be primes. The graph \(G_p(\ell)\) has the supersingular \(j\)-invariants over \(\overline{\mathbb{F}}_p\) as vertices and \(\ell\)-isogenies as edges.

Theorem 2 (Pizer). The graph \(G_p(\ell)\) is Ramanujan: \[|\lambda| \le 2\sqrt{\ell}\] for every eigenvalue \(\lambda \ne \ell + 1\).

Lemma 3. A walk of length \(O(\log p)\) mixes.

A proof

Proof (of the lemma). Expand in an eigenbasis.

  • The trivial eigenvalue gives the uniform distribution.
  • The others decay like \((2\sqrt{\ell}/(\ell+1))^k\).
